A Little About Me

I am a recent Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate who enjoys building technology that solves practical problems. My interests span software development, data analytics and artificial intelligence, with a focus on creating solutions that are functional, reliable and user-centered.

Through research projects, hackathons and collaborative development, I have gained experience turning ideas into working applications while learning to balance technical feasibility with real-world needs. Along the way, I have worked with Python, SQL, React, Firebase, Power BI and machine learning, continually expanding my skills through hands-on projects.

Beyond writing code, I enjoy leading teams, exploring new technologies and continuously improving my craft. I believe good software is built through curiosity, collaboration and thoughtful problem-solving. I am always looking for opportunities to learn, contribute and create technology that delivers meaningful impact.
